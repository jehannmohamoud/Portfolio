CASE STUDY 02 / ISLAMIC RELIEF CANADA

COMMUNITY PROGRAMS · STORYTELLING · PARTNERSHIPS
02

Make the impact
feel human.

Community programs matter most when people can see the lives behind them. At Islamic Relief Canada, I combined domestic program leadership with a communications vision grounded in community voices and lived experience.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Domestic program leadership, community partnerships, published writing and communications direction.

THE CHALLENGE

Make a growing domestic portfolio understandable and relevant, connecting the scope of the work with the people and partnerships behind it.

MY RESPONSE

Pair program knowledge with human-centred storytelling, bringing Black Muslim and other community experiences into the narrative.

EVIDENCE OF THE WORK

An annual portfolio of up to $4 million, 50 community partnerships and a published feature carrying my byline.

More on program leadership +

My domestic programs work included national campaigns, in-kind giving, corporate volunteer coordination and multi-site delivery. I also created a domestic emergency response program for natural disasters.

Environmental initiatives included tree planting, community clean-ups and recycling. Delivery required budget and vendor oversight, partner coordination and volunteer logistics.

Portfolio figures describe the scope managed, not the results of a single story or campaign.
