READ THE NUMBERS RESPONSIBLY

Context is part of the result.

Growth: (ending value − starting value) ÷ starting value × 100. A zero starting value has no defined percentage growth.

Percentage points: ending share − starting share. A rise from 2% to 15% is 13 percentage points, not 13% growth.

Annualized capacity: matches per cohort × cohorts per year. This is a calculated pace, not proof that every annual cohort reached that volume.

No double counting: annual budgets, new funding, volunteer bases, referrals and campaign reach have different scopes and may overlap. They are not added into a career-wide total.
